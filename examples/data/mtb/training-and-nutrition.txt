Mountain bike performance depends on both skills and endurance.
Two interval sessions per week can improve climbing power,
while one longer Zone 2 ride builds aerobic durability.

Strength work supports injury prevention and bike control.
Exercises like split squats, Romanian deadlifts, and core anti-rotation drills
help riders stay stable over rough terrain.

Nutrition during rides should match intensity and duration.
For sessions longer than 90 minutes, many athletes target 40-70 grams of carbohydrate per hour,
plus regular hydration with sodium in hot weather.

Recovery habits are simple but effective: refuel within one hour,
prioritize sleep, and keep easy days truly easy.
